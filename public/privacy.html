LaneLoaf is built to keep your trip data on your device by default. We keep this simple.

What LaneLoaf does

LaneLoaf imports dashcam footage and route files, extracts trip information, and shows your trips in the app. This processing is local-first.

What LaneLoaf does not do

LaneLoaf does not ask for your device location to run the core app. LaneLoaf does not run ad tracking. LaneLoaf does not sell your personal data.

Optional iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud sync, LaneLoaf can mirror your trip-library data and generated previews through your private Apple CloudKit account so your library can stay in sync across your own devices.
